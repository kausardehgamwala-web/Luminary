# Social Media Samples

Add your best social media post designs here.

## What to Include

- Instagram posts (1080x1080)
- Instagram stories (1080x1920)
- Facebook posts
- LinkedIn posts
- Twitter/X posts

## How to Add

1. Export your favorite designs from Canva as PNG/JPG
2. Name them descriptively: `instagram_product_launch.png`
3. Optionally add caption files: `instagram_product_launch_caption.txt`

## Example Structure

```
social-media/
├── instagram_summer_sale.png
├── instagram_summer_sale_caption.txt
├── facebook_new_product.png
├── linkedin_company_update.png
└── style_notes.txt
```

The more samples you add, the better the AI learns your style.
