# Advertisement Video Samples

Add your video advertisement samples here.

## What to Include

- Social media ads
- Pre-roll/mid-roll ads
- Product commercials
- Brand awareness videos

## How to Add

Include:
- Thumbnail/poster frames
- Keyframe screenshots
- Script/storyboard
- CTA examples

## Common Ad Lengths

- 6 seconds (bumper ads)
- 15 seconds (standard short)
- 30 seconds (standard)
- 60 seconds (extended)
